# Telegram Android UI Anti-Patterns

## Decorative Visuals

- Do not add glassmorphism, neumorphism, decorative shadows, ambient gradients, or floating ornaments.
- Do not create "premium" presentation layers around routine functionality.
- Do not replace flat, practical structure with aesthetic surfaces.

## Cardification

- Do not wrap ordinary settings rows or utility content in oversized cards.
- Do not use rounded panels to separate content that standard sections already separate well.
- Do not add card stacks when the rest of the app uses list and cell composition.

## Empty Minimalism

- Do not center sparse content just to make the screen look cleaner.
- Do not increase padding or row height until density disappears.
- Do not remove useful supporting information in the name of simplicity.

## Header Inflation

- Do not add hero headers, mascot illustrations, or showcase banners to practical screens.
- Do not turn settings pages into brand moments.
- Do not use oversized typography where a standard title and short summary are enough.

## Interaction Novelty

- Do not invent new gestures for common actions when Telegram or Android already has a familiar pattern.
- Do not replace direct rows and toggles with clever segmented controls or custom pickers unless a close precedent exists.
- Do not insert extra confirmations into a fast flow without a concrete safety reason.

## Modal Overuse

- Do not use a modal dialog for routine information.
- Do not add onboarding, walkthrough, or coach-mark flows for obvious features.
- Do not force multi-step setup when one screen or one row can carry the feature.

## Message-Flow Damage

- Do not loosen chat spacing to make message UI feel more "modern."
- Do not add decorative backgrounds, separators, or badges that compete with message content.
- Do not add persistent chrome that steals attention from the conversation itself.

## Typical AI-Smell in UI

- "This looks cleaner" because everything got larger, emptier, or more centered.
- "This feels modern" because it borrowed trends from unrelated apps.
- "This is more organized" because the feature became a separate destination instead of integrating locally.
